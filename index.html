Privacy Policy — Filto

Updated: 29 October 2026

Filto is a photo and video editor with a camera and local filter sharing. This policy describes the Android app's current behavior.

Your photos and videos

Filto accesses only media you choose through Android's media or document picker, media opened with Filto, and captures you take with Filto Camera. Imported files and captures are copied to the app's private storage so you can continue editing. Edits, thumbnails, My Filtos, Creator names and preferences are stored on your device. Filto does not operate a cloud photo library or user accounts.

Camera and microphone

Camera access provides live camera previews, photo/video capture and QR import. Microphone access is requested for video audio when enabled. If microphone permission is denied, you can record silent video. Filto does not use these permissions to collect recordings while you are not using these features.

Export and sharing

Media stays in Filto until you choose to save it to your device. Export creates a copy in Pictures/Filto or Movies/Filto. Sharing a Filto QR card includes the Filto name, Creator name, label color, look settings and a verification key/signature. QR cards do not contain your edited photo, video, text layers or private file paths. Anyone receiving a card can import it and see its name and Creator. Choose a Creator name you are comfortable sharing. Once you share or export a copy, deleting it inside Filto does not remove copies held by other apps or people.

Third-party components and device services

Filto uses AndroidX CameraX and Media3, ZXing and bundled Google ML Kit barcode scanning. Image and QR processing takes place on your device. This version of Filto does not request Android's Internet permission and does not include advertising or a developer-operated analytics service. According to Google's ML Kit disclosure, SDK diagnostic and usage metrics can be sent to Google over HTTPS. These include device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance, scan configuration, feature events and error codes. Filto enables QR auto-zoom suggestions; Google additionally lists a generated scanning-session ID, zoom changes and potential barcode bounding-box coordinates for this feature. The coordinates describe a barcode region in the camera image, not your geographic location. ML Kit does not send input images or decoded QR contents to Google. Google's documentation states that ML Kit does not transfer the listed metrics to third parties. The absence of Internet permission is not a promise that device services never communicate. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and https://policies.google.com/privacy for Google's disclosure and privacy practices.

Device services and media providers you choose, including Google Photos or Drive, may have their own network access and privacy policies. Filto does not control those services. Review their policies when using them.

Retention and deletion

Your projects and preferences stay on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Deleting a Library item removes its private media when no other Library item uses the same file. Duplicates can share a source file, which is retained until the last item is deleted. Already-exported media must be deleted separately from your phone's Gallery or Files app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the private Library, My Filtos, Creator preference and signing identity; export any work you want to keep first. Filto disables Android cloud backup for its private app data. This does not prevent a device manufacturer from offering its own migration services.

These local deletion actions do not request deletion of SDK metrics already received by Google. Filto has no backend or account system through which we can identify, retrieve or erase those metrics. Google's privacy practices govern their retention and available controls. The contact email below is for questions and assistance; it is not a guarantee that Google-held metrics can be deleted by us.

Security

Private media is stored in Android's app sandbox. QR payloads and built-in filter data use authenticated encryption; QR attribution uses a signing key in Android Keystore. These measures do not encrypt your exported photos/videos and do not make QR attribution a verified real-world identity. No software can guarantee absolute protection against reverse engineering or unauthorized access to a compromised device.

Children

Filto is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. It does not operate user accounts, collect age profiles or show advertisements. A person's ability to select media on their own device is controlled by Android and device settings. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact us using the address below. Media and projects stored by Filto can be removed on the device as described under Retention and deletion. We cannot remove copies already exported to other apps or shared with other people.

Changes

If Filto adds accounts, networking, advertising, developer-operated analytics or cloud services, or changes its SDK data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before those changes are released.
